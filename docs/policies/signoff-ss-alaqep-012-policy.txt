1. Purpose
To ensure all projects are commercially, operationally and contractually managed in line with practice standards through regular, structured project reviews.


2. Scope
This policy applies to:
* All live projects
* All Project Leads and project teams
* All RIBA stages


3. Policy Statement
3.1 All live projects shall be subject to periodic project reviews covering:
* Financial performance
* Resourcing
* QA and design review compliance
* Programme and delivery status
* Contractual and administrative compliance
3.2 Reviews shall be undertaken using the standard Project Review Form.
3.3 Reviews are mandatory and form part of the core project management process.


4. Responsibility
* Operations Director / Technical Director (or delegate):
 Responsible for undertaking project reviews
* Project Lead:
 Responsible for:
   * Ensuring project information is up to date
   * Supporting the review process
   * Implementing required actions


5. Review Criteria
Each review shall assess, as a minimum:
   * Contractual position and appointment status
   * Alignment of fee, invoicing and profitability
   * Accuracy of CMap data (stage, resourcing, tracker)
   * Compliance with QA and Design Review Policy
   * Resourcing and programme status
   * Change management and scope control
   * File management and documentation standards


6. Outcomes
Each project review shall result in one of the following:
   * Pass – Project is compliant and performing as expected
   * Fail – Non-compliance or risk identified
Where a project fails:
   * A non-conformity shall be raised
   * Actions, owners and timescales shall be defined
   * The Project Lead is responsible for resolving issues or delegating responsibility to their team, but ultimately the responsibility sits with the Project Lead.


7. Monitoring & Compliance
9.1 All project reviews shall be recorded using ALAQEP012a Project Review Form and saved in the Egnyte project folder.
9.3 Compliance will be reviewed periodically by the Operations Director, they will be prioritised in terms of commercial and design risk.


8. Escalation
Where identified issues are not resolved within the agreed timeframe:
   * The project will be escalated to the Board
   * Appropriate commercial or resourcing actions will be taken
