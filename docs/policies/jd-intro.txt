Ackroyd Lowrie is an ambitious, design-led architectural practice based in the vibrant creative quarter of East London. We are known for delivering high-quality, thoughtful design across a diverse range of sectors and scales, with a particular focus on residential, commercial, education and urban regeneration projects.
Our work is driven by a commitment to design excellence and sustainability, grounded in a collaborative approach that places people — clients, communities and end users — at the heart of our process. We champion the thoughtful reuse of buildings, low-energy design principles and smart technologies that improve clarity, buildability and overall project outcomes.
Innovation is central to how we work. We use advanced digital tools, including immersive 3D modelling and virtual reality, to help clients and project teams explore and refine design ideas, remove uncertainty and make informed decisions from concept through to delivery.
At Ackroyd Lowrie we value open collaboration, curiosity, and a shared ambition to shape inspiring, sustainable places to live, work and learn. We strive to be at the forefront of architectural thinking in the UK, creating buildings and neighbourhoods that enrich their context and contribute positively to the cities of tomorrow.
