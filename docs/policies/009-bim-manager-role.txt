BIM Manager Responsibilities
Role Overview
The BIM Manager is responsible for the implementation, management, and oversight of Building Information Modelling (BIM) processes across projects and within the organisation. This role ensures compliance with industry standards, drives digital innovation, supports project teams, and enhances coordination, efficiency, and data integrity throughout the project lifecycle.
The role is principally an internally focussed role. Whilst we can look to assist wider external project teams with BIM Execution Plan (BEP) implementation, where this has been requested on a project, we do not offer full BIM Management services as an organisation. If BIM Management services are required, we can assist our clients in finding a suitable party and will work closely and collaboratively with them as required.
Key Responsibilities
1. BIM Strategy &amp; Implementation
Develop and implement the company’s BIM standards, protocols, and workflows.
Lead BIM execution planning (BEP) for projects.
Ensure compliance with ISO 19650 (or relevant BIM standards).
Establish and maintain BIM templates, libraries, and documentation.
2. Project BIM Management
Oversee BIM delivery across multiple projects.
Coordinate model production and review at key project stages.
Manage Common Data Environment (CDE) processes.
Monitor model quality, compliance, and data integrity.
3. Team Leadership &amp; Support
Provide technical guidance and support to BIM Coordinators and Modellers.
Deliver BIM training and upskilling initiatives.
Promote best practice in digital design and information management.
Mentor project teams in effective BIM usage.
4. Coordination &amp; Collaboration
Liaise with consultants, contractors, and clients on BIM requirements.
Assist with interdisciplinary model coordination.
Support digital collaboration across design teams.
5. Quality &amp; Compliance
Conduct model audits and quality checks.
Assist with adherence to project EIR (Employer’s Information Requirements).
Assist with maintaining project documentation including MIDP, TIDP, BEP, and asset information requirements.
6. Digital Innovation &amp; Development
Research and implement new BIM tools and digital workflows.
Support automation within the wider studio, scripting (e.g., Dynamo, Python), AI and efficiency improvements.
Contribute to digital transformation strategy within the organisation.
