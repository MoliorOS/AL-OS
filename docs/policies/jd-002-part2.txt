Part 2 Architectural Assistant
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is an ambitious, design-led architecture practice based in the vibrant creative quarter of East London. We deliver high-quality architecture across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration projects, always committed to design excellence.
The Part 2 Architectural Assistant will work closely with senior architects and project leads, contributing across design, technical development, and project delivery. This role provides hands-on experience across the RIBA stages and supports progression toward Part 3 qualification.
Key Responsibilities
* Contribute to concept design, spatial development, and detailed design work across RIBA Stages 0–7
* Produce high-quality drawings, BIM models, and visual material to communicate design intent
* Develop technical packages in collaboration with consultants and wider project teams
* Assist with planning submissions, tender packages, and construction information
* Attend and contribute to client, consultant, and contractor meetings
* Prepare meeting minutes and assist in issuing formal project information
* Maintain and update supporting project documentation, including trackers, programmes, drawing registers, and schedules
* Support coordination across consultant teams and respond to technical queries
* Support delivery of both new-build and retrofit projects
* Manage workload effectively across multiple projects with guidance from project leads
* Contribute positively to studio culture and collaborative working

Skills & Competencies
Essential
   * Strong passion for architecture, design, and the built environment
   * Excellent design, graphical, and presentation skills
   * Solid technical understanding and attention to detail
   * Clear and confident written and verbal communication skills
   * Ability to work independently and as part of a team
   * Good organisational skills and ability to manage multiple tasks
   * Proficiency in BIM software, ideally ArchiCAD

Desirable
      * Experience using rendering and visualisation tools
      * Experience preparing or coordinating NBS specifications
      * Interest in innovative design technology and digital workflows
      * Client-facing experience

Experience
         * Minimum 1 year of professional experience following Part 1 qualification (UK experience preferred)
         * Exposure to multiple RIBA stages (0–7)
         * Experience on projects with construction values ranging from approximately £2m–£50m
         * Experience working on both retrofit and new-build projects

What We Offer
            * Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
            * Structured training and professional development opportunities, including support toward Part 3 qualification
            * Exposure to projects that are built and delivered, providing strong Part 3 case study experience
            * Ongoing BIM and software training
            * A dynamic, collaborative, and multicultural studio environment
            * Flexible working approach
            * Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
