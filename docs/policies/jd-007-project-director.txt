Project Director
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Project Director holds ultimate responsibility for the successful delivery of key projects and client relationships within the practice. This role leads major or multiple projects strategically, ensuring commercial success, design quality, and client satisfaction, while contributing to the overall leadership and growth of the business.


Key Responsibilities
Project & Commercial Leadership
* Take ultimate responsibility for the delivery of major or multiple projects across RIBA Stages 0–7
* Ensure projects are delivered to programme, budget, and quality expectations
* Oversee project financial performance, including fees, resourcing, and profitability
* Manage risk and ensure appropriate contractual and commercial controls are in place
Client Leadership
* Act as the primary senior contact for key clients
* Build long-term client relationships and secure repeat business
* Lead strategic discussions with clients, aligning design ambition with commercial objectives
Design & Delivery Oversight
* Maintain high-level oversight of design quality across projects
* Support design direction at key stages while empowering project teams
* Ensure consistency and excellence in technical delivery
Team & Leadership
* Provide leadership across project teams, supporting Associate Directors, Senior Architects, and Project Architects
* Foster a high-performing, collaborative studio culture
* Support leadership development within the team
Business & Strategic Contribution
* Play a key role in business development, including winning new work and growing client relationships
* Contribute to the strategic direction and growth of the practice
* Support financial planning, forecasting, and long-term business objectives


Skills & Competencies
Essential
* ARB/RIBA qualified Architect (Part 3)
* Proven track record of delivering complex or large-scale projects
* Strong commercial awareness and financial management skills
* Excellent client leadership and relationship management
* Strong leadership and decision-making ability
* Deep understanding of UK construction, procurement, and contracts
* High level of design and technical oversight capability
Desirable
* Strong experience in business development and securing new work
* Experience shaping studio strategy or operations
* Interest in innovation, digital design, and sustainability leadership


Experience
* Typically 10+ years post-Part 3 experience (UK preferred)
* Proven experience leading major or multiple complex projects
* Strong track record of client relationship management and repeat work
* Experience contributing to business growth and strategic direction


What We Offer
* Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
* Opportunity to take a leading role in shaping the future of the practice
* Clear pathway toward equity, directorship, or partnership
* Exposure to high-profile and complex projects
* A collaborative, ambitious, and design-led studio environment
* Flexible working approach
* Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
