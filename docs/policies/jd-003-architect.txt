Architect
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Architect will play a key role in the design and delivery of projects across all RIBA stages, taking ownership of defined work packages and supporting overall project coordination. This role represents the transition from supported delivery (Part 2) to independent responsibility, with a clear pathway toward Project Architect level.


Key Responsibilities
Design & Development
* Contribute to concept design, spatial development, and detailed design across RIBA Stages 0–7
* Develop and coordinate design packages, ensuring design intent is maintained
* Produce and oversee high-quality drawings, BIM models, and presentation material

Technical & Delivery
   * Develop technical design information and construction packages
   * Coordinate with consultants and integrate technical input into project information
   * Support delivery during construction, including responding to site queries
   * Review and check drawing information for accuracy and compliance

Project & Team Support
      * Attend and contribute to client, consultant, and contractor meetings
      * Assist in managing project programmes and deliverables
      * Maintain project documentation (drawing registers, trackers, schedules)
      * Support and guide Part 2 Architectural Assistants and apprentices

Skills & Competencies
Essential
         * ARB/RIBA qualified Architect (Part 3)
         * Strong design and technical ability, with good attention to detail
         * Ability to manage defined work packages with increasing independence
         * Clear and confident communication skills
         * Good organisational and time management skills
         * Proficiency in BIM software (ArchiCAD preferred)

Desirable
            * Some experience in construction stage and site coordination
            * Experience with NBS specifications
            * Interest in sustainable design and digital workflows
            * Experience working on both retrofit and new-build projects

Experience
               * Typically 0–3 years post-Part 3 experience (UK preferred)
               * Experience working across multiple RIBA stages
               * Exposure to construction stages and project delivery
               * Experience contributing to projects of varying scale and complexity

What We Offer
                  * Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
                  * Clear progression pathway toward Project Architect role
                  * Opportunity to take ownership of defined work packages
                  * Exposure to projects through delivery and completion
                  * Ongoing BIM and software training
                  * A collaborative, design-led studio environment
                  * Flexible working approach
                  * Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
