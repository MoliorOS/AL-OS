1. Objectives
This policy establishes a structured and consistent approach to maintenance and compliance to:
* Ensure all equipment, systems, and facilities are safe and operational
* Maintain compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
* Protect employees, visitors, and company assets
* Reduce operational risk and disruption
* Ensure maintenance activities are properly recorded and auditable


2. Scope
This policy applies to:
* Office premises and facilities
* IT equipment and systems
* Software platforms and digital infrastructure
* Health and safety equipment and compliance requirements
* Building systems including electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and gas
This policy applies to all staff and contractors involved in maintenance or compliance activities.


3. Definitions
Maintenance
 Planned and reactive activities undertaken to ensure equipment, systems, and facilities remain safe and functional.
Compliance
 Adherence to legal, regulatory, and internal requirements relating to health, safety, and operational systems.
Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM)
 Scheduled maintenance activities carried out at defined intervals.
Reactive Maintenance
 Unplanned maintenance carried out in response to faults or issues.
Compliance Records
 Documentation demonstrating that required inspections, servicing, and checks have been completed.


4. Roles and Responsibilities
Operations Director (OD)
* Provide oversight of maintenance and compliance activities
* Ensure appropriate resources and processes are in place
* Review and address significant risks or non-compliance issues


Office Manager (OM)
* Manage all maintenance and compliance activities
* Maintain the maintenance schedule and compliance tracker (Airtable)
* Coordinate contractors and service providers
* Ensure all inspections, servicing, and checks are completed on time
* Maintain compliance records and certificates on Egnyte
* Conduct or coordinate weekly checks and fire drills
* Act as the primary point of contact for maintenance issues
* Report maintenance risk or concern to OD


External Contractors
* Carry out specialist maintenance support services and compliance activities including but not limited to:
   * Fire safety servicing and inspections
   * Electrical works and inspections
   * Gas safety certification
   * Asbestos maintenance
   * IT management and support
* Provide relevant documentation to ensure competency compliance 
* Provide relevant certification and documentation following works


Employees
* Report maintenance issues promptly to the Office Manager
* Use equipment safely and in accordance with company policies
* Cooperate with maintenance and safety procedures


5. Policy Statement
5.1 All office facilities, equipment, systems, and services shall be maintained to ensure safe, compliant, and effective operation.
5.2 A planned preventative maintenance schedule shall be established and maintained to ensure all required inspections, servicing, and compliance activities are completed.
5.3 Planned maintenance activities shall include, as a minimum:
* PAT Testing (annual)
* Fire alarm and emergency lighting checks (weekly)
* Fire risk assessment (annual)
* Fire extinguisher servicing (annual)
* Emergency lighting servicing (annual)
* Gas safety certification (annual)
* Electrical inspection (EICR) (every 5 years)
* Fire drills (every 6 months)
* IT security updates (ongoing via external provider)
5.4 All maintenance and compliance activities shall be scheduled, completed, and recorded.
5.5 All compliance activities shall be tracked using approved systems, including the maintenance tracker (Airtable) and logbook records.
5.6 All compliance documentation, including certificates and reports, shall be stored centrally and maintained in an accurate, up-to-date, and auditable format.
5.7 All employees are responsible for reporting maintenance issues promptly through appropriate communication channels.
5.8 Reported issues shall be assessed, prioritised, and resolved, with health and safety risks addressed immediately.
5.9 External contractors shall be suitably qualified and competent, and shall provide certification upon completion of works.
5.10 IT systems and security shall be maintained by the appointed external provider to ensure systems are secure, up to date, and operational.
5.11 No equipment or system shall be used where it is known to be unsafe or non-compliant.
5.12 Any identified compliance risks or failures shall be escalated and addressed promptly.
5.13 Maintenance and compliance records shall be retained to support audit, review, and legal requirements.
5.14 Maintenance and compliance activities shall be reviewed periodically, and lessons learned shall inform continuous improvement.
5.15 Compliance with this policy is mandatory.


6. Process
6.1 The maintenance compliance process includes:
* Scheduling and tracking of planned maintenance
* Completion of required inspections and servicing
* Recording and storing compliance documentation
* Reporting and resolving maintenance issues
* Ongoing monitoring and review
6.2 The Airtable maintenance tracker and compliance logbook shall be used to ensure all actions are completed and recorded.
