Project Architect
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Project Architect is responsible for the successful delivery of projects from inception through to completion. This role leads project teams, manages client relationships, and ensures projects are delivered to programme, budget, and quality standards. It represents a step into full project ownership and leadership within the practice.


Key Responsibilities
Project Leadership
* Lead projects across all RIBA Stages 0–7, taking full responsibility for delivery
* Develop project strategies, programmes, and resourcing in collaboration with senior staff
* Ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to the required quality

Design & Technical Oversight
   * Lead and coordinate design development, maintaining design quality throughout
   * Oversee production of technical information and construction packages
   * Ensure coordination across all consultant disciplines
   * Review and sign off project information for issue

Client & Stakeholder Management
      * Act as the primary point of contact for clients, consultants, and contractors
      * Lead project meetings and manage stakeholder communication
      * Build and maintain strong client relationships

Construction & Delivery
         * Lead construction stage activities, including contract administration
         * Oversee site progress, quality control, and issue resolution
         * Manage responses to RFIs, variations, and technical queries

Team Leadership & Practice Contribution
            * Lead and mentor project team members, including Architects, Part 2s, and apprentices
            * Delegate tasks effectively and support team development
            * Contribute to studio culture and continuous improvement of practice processes

Skills & Competencies
Essential
               * ARB/RIBA qualified Architect (Part 3)
               * Proven ability to lead and deliver projects through all RIBA stages
               * Strong technical and detailing knowledge
               * Excellent client-facing and stakeholder management skills
               * Strong project management and organisational ability
               * Ability to lead and coordinate multidisciplinary teams
               * Proficiency in BIM software (ArchiCAD preferred)

Desirable
                  * Strong experience in contract administration and site delivery
                  * Experience managing project programmes and budgets
                  * Experience with NBS specifications and technical documentation
                  * Interest in innovation, digital design tools, and sustainable design

Experience
                     * Typically 3+ years post-Part 3 experience (UK preferred)
                     * Proven experience delivering projects through construction
                     * Experience leading projects of varying scale and complexity
                     * Experience working across both retrofit and new-build projects

What We Offer
                        * Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
                        * Opportunity to lead projects and take full ownership of delivery
                        * Clear progression pathway toward Senior Architect and leadership roles
                        * Exposure to a diverse portfolio of built work
                        * Ongoing BIM and professional development support
                        * A collaborative, design-led studio environment
                        * Flexible working approach
                        * Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
