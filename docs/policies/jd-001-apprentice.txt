Architectural Assistant Apprentice
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is an innovative, design-led architecture practice based in East London. We are committed to design excellence across a range of sectors and scales, creating inspiring spaces for living, working, and leisure while contributing positively to the cities of the future.
The Architectural Assistant Apprentice will support the design team while working towards formal architectural qualifications. This role offers hands-on experience across a variety of projects and direct learning from experienced architects.


Key Responsibilities
* Assist in preparing architectural designs and drawings using ArchiCAD
* Contribute to conceptual designs, project proposals, and construction documentation
* Collaborate with the project team to develop 3D models and visualisations
* Participate in site visits to gain practical construction and technical experience
* Prepare reports, schedules, and documentation for internal and client meetings
* Maintain and update supporting project documentation, including trackers, meeting minutes, drawing registers, and schedules
* Research materials, building regulations, and sustainable design solutions
* Participate actively in team meetings and design discussions

Skills & Competencies
Essential
   * Passion for architecture, design, and urban development
   * Eagerness to learn and develop new skills
   * Basic design, graphical, and technical skills
   * Strong communication skills and good timekeeping
   * Ability to work effectively in a team environment
   * Interest in innovative technology and sustainable design solutions
   * Eligibility for acceptance onto an Architectural Assistant Apprenticeship university course

Desirable
      * Basic knowledge of ArchiCAD or other design software

Experience & Training
         * No prior professional experience required; this is a training role
         * All necessary skills will be taught as part of the apprenticeship, including BIM and software training

What We Offer
            * Structured training and professional development opportunities
Hands-on experience across multiple project types
            * BIM and software training provided as standard
            * Friendly, flexible, and multicultural studio environment
            * Commitment to sustainability and equal opportunities
