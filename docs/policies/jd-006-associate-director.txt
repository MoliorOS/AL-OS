Associate Director
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Associate Director plays a key leadership role within the practice, combining project oversight, team leadership, and strategic input into studio operations. This role is responsible for ensuring the successful delivery of multiple projects, supporting business development, and contributing to the long-term growth and direction of the practice.


Key Responsibilities
Project Oversight & Delivery
* Oversee multiple projects across RIBA Stages 0–7, ensuring successful delivery in line with programme, budget, and quality
* Provide strategic direction to Project Architects and Senior Architects
* Monitor project performance, including programme, resourcing, and risk
* Intervene in complex project challenges to support successful outcomes
Design & Technical Leadership
* Maintain oversight of design quality across projects, ensuring alignment with the practice’s design ethos
* Support design reviews and provide high-level input at key project stages
* Oversee technical quality and consistency across project outputs
Client & Business Development
* Build and maintain strong client relationships, acting as a senior point of contact
* Identify and support new business opportunities, including repeat work and new clients
* Contribute to bids, pitches, and fee proposals
Team Leadership & Studio Management
* Lead and mentor Senior Architects, Project Architects, and wider teams
* Support team development, performance management, and career progression
* Contribute to resourcing decisions and studio planning
Practice Contribution
* Support the development of studio processes, standards, and strategic initiatives
* Contribute to financial awareness across projects, including fee management and profitability
* Play an active role in shaping the growth and direction of the practice


Skills & Competencies
Essential
* ARB/RIBA qualified Architect (Part 3)
* Proven ability to oversee multiple projects and teams
* Strong leadership and team management skills
* Excellent client-facing and stakeholder management abilities
* Strong understanding of project delivery, programme, and commercial considerations
* High level of design and technical competence
* Proficiency in BIM software (ArchiCAD preferred)
Desirable
* Experience contributing to business development and winning work
* Experience managing project financial performance
* Strong understanding of contracts and procurement routes
* Interest in innovation, digital workflows, and sustainable design


Experience
* Typically 7–10+ years post-Part 3 experience (UK preferred)
* Proven experience leading multiple projects and teams
* Experience managing complex projects through construction
* Experience supporting business development and client growth


What We Offer
* Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
* Clear pathway toward Director level and long-term leadership within the practice
* Opportunity to shape studio operations and strategic direction
* Exposure to a diverse portfolio of projects and clients
* Ongoing professional development and leadership opportunities
* A collaborative, design-led, and multicultural studio environment
* Flexible working approach
* Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
