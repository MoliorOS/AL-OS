Architectural Technician  
Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Architectural Technician plays a key role in the technical development and delivery of projects, specialising in detailed design, BIM modelling, and construction information. This role focuses on ensuring technical accuracy, compliance, and buildability across all stages, working closely with Architects and Project Architects to support successful project delivery.


Key Responsibilities
Technical Design & Delivery
* Produce detailed technical drawings and construction information across RIBA Stages 3–6
* Develop and coordinate technical packages with a strong focus on buildability and compliance
* Prepare high-quality 2D drawings and detailed information at scales including 1:10, 1:5, and 1:2
* Support the delivery of projects on site, including responding to technical queries
BIM & Modelling
* Develop and manage detailed 3D BIM models using ArchiCAD (preferred) or equivalent software
* Ensure accuracy, coordination, and consistency of BIM information across project teams
* Work within established BIM workflows and contribute to model development from early stages
Coordination & Collaboration
* Coordinate with consultants, suppliers, subcontractors, and contractors to develop construction information
* Support integration of specialist design input into project documentation
* Work collaboratively with Architects and Project Architects to resolve technical challenges
Compliance & Quality
* Ensure all technical information complies with UK Building Regulations and relevant legislation
* Apply knowledge of CDM 2015 and Building Safety Act 2022 requirements
* Contribute to quality assurance processes, ensuring accuracy and consistency of information
Practice & Project Support
* Work independently to deliver packages of information while understanding the wider project context
* Manage workload effectively to meet project programmes and deadlines
* Contribute positively to a collaborative and technically strong studio environment


Skills & Competencies
Essential
* Strong technical and detailing ability, with experience producing construction information
* Excellent 2D drafting skills
* High level of proficiency in BIM modelling (ArchiCAD preferred; Revit or AutoCAD acceptable)
* Strong understanding of UK Building Regulations and compliance requirements
* Knowledge of CDM 2015 and Building Safety Act 2022
* Ability to work independently and manage technical packages
* Strong organisational skills and ability to meet deadlines
Desirable
* Experience working across a range of project scales and sectors
* Experience coordinating with contractors, suppliers, and subcontractors
* Understanding of specification writing (e.g. NBS)
* Interest in digital workflows and BIM development


Experience
* Typically 3–5 years’ professional experience post qualification
* Relevant qualification in Architectural Technology, Architecture, or related discipline
* Experience working in BIM-led project environments from early design through to construction
* Experience contributing to projects across multiple RIBA stages, particularly technical and delivery stages


What We Offer
* Competitive salary, commensurate with experience 
* Opportunity to develop technical expertise within a design-led practice
* Exposure to a range of project types and scales
* Ongoing BIM and software training
* A collaborative, design-led, and multicultural studio environment
* Flexible working approach
* Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
