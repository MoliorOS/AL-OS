Senior Architect


Role Overview
Ackroyd Lowrie is a design-led architecture practice based in East London, delivering high-quality projects across residential, commercial, education, and regeneration sectors. We are committed to design excellence, innovation, and the use of digital tools to enhance both design and client engagement.
The Senior Architect plays a key leadership role within the practice, responsible for overseeing multiple projects, supporting delivery quality across the studio, and mentoring team members. This role combines project leadership with strategic input into design, technical standards, and studio operations, and represents a clear progression toward Associate level.


Key Responsibilities
Project & Design Leadership
* Lead multiple projects across RIBA Stages 0–7, ensuring successful delivery from inception to completion
* Provide strategic oversight of design development, maintaining design quality across projects
* Support Project Architects in leading project teams and resolving complex design and technical challenges
Technical Oversight & Quality Assurance
* Oversee technical design quality across projects, ensuring compliance with UK regulations and best practice
* Review and approve project information, including detailed drawings and construction packages
* Lead on quality assurance processes across the studio, improving consistency and delivery standards
* Provide guidance on complex detailing and technical problem-solving
Client & Stakeholder Management
* Build and maintain strong client relationships across multiple projects
* Support Project Architects in managing client expectations, programme, and delivery
* Lead key meetings and contribute to business development opportunities where appropriate
Team Leadership & Mentorship
* Mentor and support Architects, Part 2s, and apprentices in their professional development
* Provide guidance, feedback, and technical support across project teams
* Foster a collaborative, design-led studio culture
Practice Contribution
* Contribute to the development of studio processes, standards, and workflows
* Support resourcing and project planning across the practice
* Play an active role in shaping the growth and direction of the studio


Skills & Competencies
Essential
* ARB/RIBA qualified Architect (Part 3)
* Strong design, technical, and detailing expertise
* Proven ability to lead multiple projects and support wider team delivery
* Excellent knowledge of UK building regulations and construction processes
* Strong client-facing and stakeholder management skills
* Excellent organisational and leadership skills
* Ability to mentor and support team members effectively
* Proficiency in BIM software (ArchiCAD preferred)
Desirable
* Strong experience in contract administration and construction stage delivery
* Experience implementing quality assurance processes
* Experience with NBS specifications and technical documentation
* Interest in digital workflows, BIM development, and innovation
* Interest in sustainable and low-carbon design approaches


Experience
* Typically 5+ years post-Part 3 experience (UK preferred)
* Proven experience leading multiple projects through construction
* Experience managing project teams and supporting junior staff
* Experience across a range of project scales and sectors, including both retrofit and new-build


What We Offer
* Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
* Clear pathway toward Associate and leadership roles within the practice
* Opportunity to shape project delivery standards and studio processes
* Exposure to a diverse portfolio of built work
* Ongoing BIM and professional development support
* A collaborative, design-led, and multicultural studio environment
* Flexible working approach
* Commitment to sustainability, equality, and inclusive working
