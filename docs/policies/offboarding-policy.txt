1. Objectives
This policy establishes a structured and consistent offboarding process to:
* Ensure a smooth and professional exit for all employees
* Protect company assets, data, and systems
* Maintain compliance with legal and financial obligations
* Capture feedback to support continuous improvement
* Ensure continuity of work and minimise disruption


2. Scope
This policy applies to:
* All employees leaving the practice (resignation, termination, or end of contract)
* All departments involved in offboarding activities
* All stages of offboarding including pre-leaving, final day, and post-departure


3. Definitions
Offboarding
The formal process of managing an employee’s exit from the organisation.
Final Pay
The final salary payment including any outstanding salary, accrued holiday, or deductions.
Exit Interview
A structured discussion conducted prior to departure to gather feedback on the employee experience.
Company Assets
All physical and digital property belonging to the organisation (e.g. laptops, keys, software access).


4. Roles and Responsibilities
Operations Director (OD)
* Send formal acknowledgement of resignation
* Communicate leaver details to Ops team
* Conduct or delegate exit interviews
* Review feedback and identify improvement actions
* Approve any exceptional offboarding arrangements
* Ensure continuity of project resources


Finance Manager (FM)
* Process final salary payments
* Issue P45 documentation
* Update financial and payroll records
* Ensure recovery or reconciliation of any financial items
* Action leave dates on financial management systems


Office Manager (OM)
* Oversee and coordinate the offboarding process end-to-end
* Manage return of company equipment and assets
* Remove access to systems and accounts
* Update internal records and systems
* Coordinate IT offboarding with external providers (e.g. Dr Logic)


Line Manager (LM)
* Manage handover of work and responsibilities
* Support knowledge transfer
* Ensure continuity of project delivery
* Provide feedback where relevant
5. Process
5.1 The offboarding process follows a structured workflow including:
* Notification and initiation of offboarding
* Pre-departure preparation and asset tracking
* Final day procedures
* IT and systems deactivation
* Financial closure
* Post-departure review and documentation
5.2 The offboarding checklist shall be used to ensure consistency and completeness of the process.
6. Policy Statement
6.1 All employee departures shall be formally documented, with the leaving date agreed and recorded.
6.2 A structured offboarding process shall be followed for all employees to ensure continuity of work and compliance with company requirements.
6.3 All responsibilities, project work, and knowledge shall be appropriately handed over prior to the employee’s departure.
6.4 All company property, including equipment, keys, and access devices, shall be returned prior to or on the employee’s final working day.
6.5 All systems access shall be removed or restricted promptly upon departure, including but not limited to:
* Email and communication systems
* Financial and project systems
* File storage platforms
* Design and software tools
6.6 External IT providers shall ensure secure deactivation of accounts and appropriate handling of company data.
6.7 All employee records, systems, and internal directories shall be updated to reflect the departure.
6.8 Final salary payments, including any outstanding holiday entitlement, shall be processed in accordance with company procedures, and all statutory documentation (including P45) shall be issued.
6.9 All company assets shall be verified upon return, and any discrepancies or damage shall be escalated appropriately.
6.10 An exit interview shall be conducted for all employees where reasonably practicable.
6.11 Exit interviews shall, as a minimum, seek to:
* Gather feedback on employee experience
* Identify areas for organisational improvement
* Understand reasons for departure
6.12 Feedback from exit interviews shall be reviewed and used to inform continuous improvement of company processes and practices.
6.13 All offboarding actions shall be completed, recorded, and formally closed.
6.14 Compliance with this policy is mandatory.
