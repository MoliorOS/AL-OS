## 1. Strategic Objectives & Thought Leadership
* **Primary Goal**: To become "Thought Leaders" by building a consistent audience [cite: 14, 16].
* **Channels**:
    * **Monthly Podcast** [cite: 20].
    * **Daily LinkedIn posts** [cite: 20].
    * **Weekly Substack newsletter** [cite: 21].
* **Personal Branding**: The aim is to build the personal brands of all company members to represent the culture online [cite: 22, 23].

## 2. The Sales Funnel (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU)
* **TOFU (Top of Funnel)**: Focus on growing the audience via infographics, "how-to" architectural content, and sustainability statistics [cite: 76, 167, 169, 172].
* **MOFU (Middle of Funnel)**: Build trust through culture-related content [cite: 77, 177].
* **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)**: Drive calls to action via project announcements, lead magnets (White Papers/Apps), and direct outreach [cite: 78, 178-183].

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
The plan targets three distinct segments:
1.  **London-based Developers**: Specifically those building 100-300 homes (e.g., Berkeley Group) [cite: 49, 50].
2.  **Alternative/Sustainable Developers**: Firms like Pocket or Bywater [cite: 54, 55].
3.  **Commercial Retrofit**: Developers focused on hotels or office retrofits [cite: 60, 62].

## 4. Sales Matrix: The "Who, How, When"
The document maps a 5-step process from Identify to Win [cite: 85-104]:
* **Identify/Invite**: Use recommendations, Pipedrive, and personal branding [cite: 112, 117, 126].
* **First Contact (Event)**: Invite targets to "Monthly Breakfasts" or hospitality events (Lunches, Cricket, West Ham matches) [cite: 120-122].
* **Pitch**: Listen to "pain points" and offer relevant solutions [cite: 123].
* **Bid**: Provide brochures with the client's logo, feasibility studies, and area schedules [cite: 138-141].
* **Win**: Execute the project and "always be listening for pain points" for future work [cite: 145, 146].
