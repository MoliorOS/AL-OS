ABOUT ACKROYD LOWRIE

Ackroyd Lowrie is a leading architectural and interior 
design firm. It was founded by Jon Ackroyd and Oliver 
Lowrie in 2014 with a shared vision to design inspiring 
places to live, work and play. Over the past ten years, Jon and Oliver have built 
the company’s reputation for high quality design 
and placemaking, with a specialism in retrofit and 
regeneration. 
The practice works on projects across a variety of 
sectors, with the majority of clients being within 
the property sector, and exclusively undertakes 
projects for professional clients. Whilst the majority 
of the practice’s clients are business professionals, 
Ackroyd Lowrie’s reputation for education, film and 
photographic projects has attracted end user clients 
in these sectors to work with the company. 

SERVICES
The practice generally, but not exclusively undertakes 
the following services: 
- Feasibility studies
- Pre-Applications
- Planning Applications
- Masterplanning
- Technical Design
- Project Management
- Interior Design
SECTORS
The practice works generally, but not exclusively in the 
following sectors:

BUSINESS PLAN
Our business plan for the next 2 years (1yr into 3yr 
cycle) is to pursue stability and profitability ahead 
turnover growth, and focus on bolstering our 
competancy to gain advantage of the increased 
focus on competent design. 

The company mission remains to ‘build a company 
that defines the future of cities, putting people at 
the heart of the buildings we design’, and business 
plan that sits below that has been adapted to the 
particular circumstances of 2025. 

Ackroyd Lowrie have managed the tough financial 
market in the property sector from 2022-24 by careful 
planning, and maintaining sales via a well developed 
Marketing and Business Development strategy. 
SInce last year, AL have grown revenue and profit, 
and have an improved pipeline versus the prvious 
year. This is a reflection of slightly improved condition 
in the property market post-election, and AL’s 
continued active marketing and lead generation. 

Our business plan for the next 12-24 months is to plan 
for gentrle growth, and focus on further bolsterign 
our competencies though strategic hiring, and the  
developement of our internal processes in relation 
to the Building Safety Act, and raft of legislation 
changes in relation to fire. 

Moreover, after the successful sale of a development 
site that AL bought in an SVP, the business is in a 
strong financial position, with increased working 
capital to allow us to invest in growth for the future.

BUSINESS PLAN 2025
Each year AL undertakes a performance review of 
the previous calendar /  company year and develops a 
strategic plan for the coming year. 
We identify key challenges, risks and opportunities 
and reference these against a wider 3+  year plan . 
This in turn helps us set our overall objectives. 
Following the end of year review in December 2024, 
the directors identified three driving factors that 
they believe will impact our business over the next 12 
months.

1. MARKET /  MACRO ECONOMICS
There has been some positive movement in the 
market with interest rates stabilising, inflation 
dropping and build costs and materials also returning 
to lower levels- however we see the period of 
economic stagnation in the market lasting until Q3 
2024 at least. 
We partner with Capital Economics who provide 
regular updates on the state of the market which are 
useful in guiding our predictions.
Given the prediction for a low growth economy in 
2024, the business is focusing on financial policies 
that accord to this challenge: 
- Focus on collections/  cashflow to sure up cash 
position
- Identify clients with potential liquidity problems and 
avoid working for them with ‘go/no-go’ list
- Trim costs where possible through better 
procurement, reduction of unnecessary purchases, 
and a decision to maintain current headcount (not 
increase or decrease).

2. REGULATORY CHANGE
There has been significant changes in the legislative 
framework in our industry, including but not limited 
to, the Building Safety Act, Fire Safety Act and 
new Building Regulations, and increasing focus on 
Sustainable/  ESG requirements. 
Ackroyd Lowrie have focused on training staff to gain 
expert knowledge of this new framework to give us a 
comparative edge against those slower to react. AL’s 
knowledge around sustainability and focus on retrofit 
and regeneration is proving effective, and we are 
seeing an up-tick in clients approaching us regarding 
new projects.- Focus on profitability on all projects 
- Maintain effective marketing strategy to give 
competitive edge in shrinking market. 

3. PROCESSES 
Given the context of a tough market, and new 
legislative framework. Ackroyd Lowrie are committed 
to strengthening and codifying our processes to 
ensure best practice in regards to compliance and 
competency.  The processes have been split up into 
those relating to the company, and those that are 
applied to the way that we run our projects, with 
workshops and best practice guidance established 
around these. As well as strategic hires, and 
strengthening our internal processes, AL have signed 
up to achieve ISO 9001 and 140001 by 2025.

ROLES AND PROTOCOLS       
SENIOR TEAM 
In the AL structure there is a senior team of 5 that 
report to the directors. This senior team is comprised 
of 2 project directors, The Head of Technical, one 
associate, and the finance manager. Each of the 
technical members of the senior team has multiple 
projects at different stages, and requiring different 
levels of resourcing from within their team. 
At the start of every month, the senior team meets 
with the directors and the finance manager to map 
out the month’s deliverables on each project, and 
the resources required to achieve these deliverables- 
these are checked back against the fee available. 
There is then a weekly meeting, with the senior team 
and directors, to check the progress against the plan 
and make adjustments where required. 
Below each of the seniors, there is a team of 
Architects/Architectural Assistants. At the start 
of every week, there is a ‘start the week’  meeting 
where the overview of what we need to achieve is set 
out. Wednesday afternoons are dedicated to design 
reviews. The seniors will liaise with their team to 
ensure drawings and trackers are up to date and then 
present to the director in charge. These reviews occur 
at key milestones, and are mainly focused on design 
and deliverables. They sit separate from the quality 
assurance process. 

OPERATIONS TEAM
The operations team is overseen by the Finance 
Manager who manages the day to day aspects of 
running the business alongside the Office Manager.
This covers everything from finance, IT, office 
operations to the two PAs who support the directors. 
The purpose of this team is to free up the designers 
and directors to focus on projects. 


COVID-19 
The pandemic impacted all businesses in the UK. 
Initially AL’s revenue and sales reduced in the early 
period of COVID, but rapidly increased in the later 
stages. 
The challenges of Covid however, have enabled AL 
to develop and refine it’s online systems and working 
capacity. All AL systems are now on the cloud. Virtual 
meetings are regular and Zoom groups are used to 
communicate informally. This has actually pushed 
forward plans and improved efficiencies in many places.

HEALTH SAFETY & HR 
AL has a 5yr contract in place with Peninsula, an 
award-winning expert HR and Health and Safety 
company which provides extensive support to AL and 
ensures AL follows best practice. 
This includes ensuring AL has up-to-date health & 
safety plans, employment contracts, staff handbooks 
and assists the operations team in providing all 
supporting information and changes in regulations. 
They advise on necessary testing and support a regular 
check-up. 
In terms of Covid-19 guidance checklists and checks to 
support the operations team and ensure AL worked in 
a safe and effective manor throughout the Pandemic.
