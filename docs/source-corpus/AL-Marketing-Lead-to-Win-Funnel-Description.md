# AL Marketing — Lead-to-Win Funnel: Detailed Process Description

This document provides a detailed text description of the **AL Marketing — Lead-to-Win Funnel** flow chart, mapping the journey from brand awareness to project completion [cite: 1].

## 1. Awareness and Outreach (Top of Funnel)
The process begins with high-level activities designed to build brand visibility and identify potential leads [cite: 1]:
* **Primary Objective:** Establish brand awareness and initiate outreach [cite: 1].
* **Content Strategy:** Daily LinkedIn posts are used to develop brand awareness [cite: 1].
* **Direct Outreach:** Daily LinkedIn outreach targets leads across the Top-of-Funnel (TOFU), Middle-of-Funnel (MOFU), and Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU) stages [cite: 1].
* **CRM Management:** Identified leads are systematically added to the CRM for tracking [cite: 1].

## 2. Engagement and Nurturing (Middle of Funnel)
Once a lead is identified, the funnel moves into engagement through visual connections and educational content [cite: 1]:
* **Networking:** The flow involves establishing visual connections on LinkedIn and viewing profiles to understand lead interests [cite: 1].
* **Connection Requests:** Formal requests to connect are sent to build the network [cite: 1].
* **Lead Magnets and Channels:** Leads are funneled through specific content channels including:
    * Monthly Webinars [cite: 1].
    * Weekly LinkedIn Newsletters [cite: 1].
    * Case Studies (e.g., "Old Projects - Case Study / Modern Floor") [cite: 1].
* **Conversion Points:** These activities lead to "Lead Magnet Downloads" or newsletter subscriptions, moving the lead deeper into the funnel [cite: 1].

## 3. Evaluation and Initial Offers
This phase focuses on direct interaction and assessing the potential for a formal partnership [cite: 1]:
* **Service Delivery (DELIVER):** Emphasizes doing "an excellent job" on existing tasks to build trust [cite: 1].
* **Audit and Learning:** The team assesses learnings for the prospect's brand and offers a "Free Tech Audit" or solution review [cite: 1].
* **Fit Assessment:** A decision point asks, "Are they the right fit?" [cite: 1]:
    * **If No:** The lead is moved to a "Keep Nurturing" track [cite: 1].
    * **If Yes:** They proceed toward the formal pitch stage [cite: 1].

## 4. Pitching and Winning (Bottom of Funnel)
The final stages are geared toward closing the deal and starting the project [cite: 1]:
* **Presentation:** Preparation and delivery of a "CUSTOMER PRESENTATION" [cite: 1].
* **Pitch Meeting:** A formal meeting conducted in-office or via Zoom [cite: 1].
* **Internal Alignment:** Includes a "LUNCH - Internal RE-START / next steps" box for team coordination [cite: 1].
* **Free Feasibility Study:** For promising leads, a free feasibility study is conducted and presented back to the prospect [cite: 1].
* **Final Decision:** The process concludes with a "Decision to proceed?" check [cite: 1].
* **The Win:** Successful completion of these steps results in a final project win [cite: 1].

## 5. Process Legends and Tools
* **Role Box Colour Legend:** Categorizes different box colors by their role or function in the funnel [cite: 1].
* **Visual Flow:** Uses arrows to denote the progression of leads from initial contact through to the final contract [cite: 1].
