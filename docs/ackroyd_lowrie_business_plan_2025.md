## 3. Macro Challenges & Strategic Response Policies

### 1. Macro Environment & Market Stagnation
* **Diagnosis:** Challenging property market conditions driven by high interest rates and inflation, with low growth expected through 2024. Market insights are updated regularly via partnership with Capital Economics.
* **Guiding Policy:** Focus on cash collection, screen prospective clients for liquidity risks using a strict 'go/no-go' list, trim procurement costs, maintain steady headcount (~20 staff), and outperform competitors via active thought leadership.

### 2. Regulatory Reform (Building Safety Act 2022 & Fire Safety)
* **Diagnosis:** Sweeping legislative changes—including the Fire Safety Act 2021, Building Safety Act 2022, amendments to Approved Document B, and the Building Regulations 2023—create operational complexity but present opportunities for technical market differentiation.
* **Guiding Policy:** Build internal expertise around compliance, train all staff on PAS 8671 and BSI Flex 8670 standards, actively participate in the Architectural Technical Leads Group (ATLG), and lead marketing campaigns focused on retrofit and sustainability.

### 3. Process Efficiency & ISO Standards
* **Diagnosis:** Tighter economic conditions and complex regulations require complete codification of internal quality control processes.
* **Guiding Policy:** Partnered with Automated Data Driven Design (ADDD / Alister Lewis) to overhaul document management, standardized folder structures, and BIM/ISO 19650 drawing standards. Targeted full ISO 9001 (Quality Management) and ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) accreditation by 2025.

2. **Part 1 Architectural Assistant:** Develops CAD/BIM drafting, basic planning principles, and initial tracker input.
3. **Part 2 Architectural Assistant:** Sets up VR models, housing design standards, capacity studies, and draft minutes under supervision.
4. **Architect:** Demonstrates technical proficiency across CAD/BIM/Adobe, basic Building Regulations, and project leading on smaller tasks.
5. **Project Architect:** Acts as creative decision-maker, manages trackers, leads smaller Design Team Meetings (DTMs), and guides clients through VR.
6. **Associate:** Focuses on design leadership, fee proposal drafting, team resourcing, Stage 4 QA packs, and client relationship management.
7. **Associate Director:** Leads core creative strategies, fee writing, team resourcing, public consultations, and new business acquisition.
8. **Director:** Sets company-wide design strategies, manages board-level governance, oversees corporate culture, and secures major strategic accounts.

## 7. Project Process, Delivery & Compliance

### Project Management & Fee Proposals
* **CMAP Integration:** All projects, fee proposals, and resource allocations are generated and monitored in CMAP.
* **Resource Mapping:** Senior leads map required staff hours and deliverables against available fee budgets prior to issuing proposals.
* **DocuSign Contracting:** Fee proposals and standard conditions are automatically compiled and issued via DocuSign; no live project work commences without signed contracts and cashflow forecasts.

### Standard Legal Terms & Risk Mitigation
To protect against changes in the insurance and professional indemnity (PI) market, AL uses legal terms customized by Trowers & Hamlins:
1. **Fire Exemption Clauses:** Explicit liability exclusions tied directly to Professional Indemnity insurance cover limits.
2. **Basement Exclusions:** Mandates that structural and basement specialists are contracted directly by the client.
3. **Debt Collection Protocols:** Enhanced payment terms and structured deposit triggers to protect cash flow.
4. **Bespoke Contracts:** Any client-issued bespoke agreements or collateral warranties must be reviewed and approved by AL's solicitors and insurers before execution.

### RIBA Lifecycle Execution

#### Stage 0–1: Feasibility
* Briefing workshops (designed in consultation with Steve Smith, former Head of Masterplanning at Farrells).
* Deployment of standard capacity, pre-app, and feasibility templates.
* Project tracker initialization.

#### Stage 2–3: Planning
* Early coordination with fire engineers, building control, and planning specialists.
* Design verification and client sign-off completed using Virtual Reality (VR) walkthroughs and formal Stage Reports.

#### Stage 3–4: Technical Design
* All drawings produced using standardized ArchiCAD templates.
* Structural, fire, and waterproofing specialists must be directly engaged by the client.
* Cross-director Quality Assurance (QA) checks required for all stage packages.

#### Stage 5–7: Construction & Post-Occupancy
* **Scope Exclusion:** AL explicitly does **not** undertake Contract Administration or direct Project Management on new construction sites. Projects must be managed by the developer client, an independent PM, or a main contractor.
* **Site Services:** AL provides site inspection support tools, periodic site visits, and post-occupancy evaluation reviews.

### Compliance Project Trackers
Every active project relies on a multi-tab Master Excel Tracker created by the project Associate:
* **BRIEF:** Captures initial scope, appointment details, agreed fee schedules, and milestone deliverables.
* **CLIENT & CONSULTANT TEAM:** Operates as a live project directory and prompts required specialist roles.
* **COMPLIANCE CHECK-LISTS:** Compiles all planning conditions and building control items into actionable DTM agendas.
* **STAGE CHECK-LISTS:** Stage-by-stage prompts to ensure necessary pre-app, planning, tender, and site actions are completed.
* **DELIVERABLES / DRAWING ISSUE SHEET:** Standardized drawing release schedules tailored to the fee proposal.
* **STAGE REPORTS:** Automated exports compiled to formally re-confirm scope and design decisions with clients.

## 8. ISO 19650 Drawing Naming & Document Protocols

In partnership with ADDD, AL enforces an ISO 19650-compliant drawing syntax across all ArchiCAD projects.

### File Naming Syntax
`[Project Code] - [Originator] - [Zone] - [Level] - [Form] - [Discipline] - [Package][Number] _ [Title]`

* **Full Worked Example:** `1500-ALA-XX-00-DR-A-01100_Existing Ground Floor Plan`

### Field Code Definitions

| Identifier Category | Code Options | Description / Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Identifiers** | `1500`<br>`ALA`<br>`A` | • Project Code (4-digit numeric)<br>• Originator Code (Ackroyd Lowrie Architects)<br>• Discipline Code (Architectural) |
| **Zone Classification** | `XX`<br>`ZZ`<br>`NB` | • No specific zone<br>• Multiple zones<br>• Defined by BIM Execution Plan |
| **Spatial Level** | `B2` / `B1`<br>`00`<br>`0M`<br>`01` / `1M` | • Second / First Basement<br>• Ground Floor<br>• Ground Floor Mezzanine<br>• First Floor / First Floor Mezzanine |
| **Form / Type Identifier** | `BQ`<br>`CM`<br>`DA`<br>`DR`<br>`M2` / `M3`<br>`RP`<br>`SA` / `SH` | • Bill of Quantities<br>• Contract Management Document<br>• Data Sheet<br>• 2D Drawing<br>• 2D / 3D Model File<br>• Report<br>• Schedule of Accommodation / Schedule |
| **Work Package** | `99`<br>`10`<br>`00`<br>`01`<br>`05`<br>`18`<br>`21` / `22` / `23` | • Sketch Package<br>• Planning Package<br>• General Arrangement<br>• Demolition & Existing<br>• Area Schedules<br>• Access & Maintenance<br>• External Walls / Internal Walls / Floors |
