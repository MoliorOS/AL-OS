## Practice voice (from the firm's own introduction)

> "Ackroyd Lowrie is an ambitious, design-led architectural practice based in the vibrant
> creative quarter of East London. We are known for delivering high-quality, thoughtful design
> across a diverse range of sectors and scales... Our work is driven by a commitment to design
> excellence and sustainability, grounded in a collaborative approach that places people —
> clients, communities and end users — at the heart of our process."

Themes worth keeping consistent in any AL-facing writing this repo produces: thoughtful reuse of
buildings, low-energy design, smart technologies (immersive 3D, VR) used to remove uncertainty
and support informed decisions, collaboration and curiosity as stated values.

## Demand generation model

TOFU/MOFU/BOFU concentric-circle strategy. Stated target: **2 bids per month**. Operating
principle: **"free stuff wins work."**

## Channels

- **BCB — Breakfast Club Briefings**, monthly, organised by Christian (Eversleigh retainer,
  ~£36k/yr) — people whose job is to go out and bring in leads. Attendance lives in a Google
  Sheet that doesn't always talk to Pipedrive.
- **Urban Forecast podcast** — 52+ episodes, used deliberately as a way to interview
  organisations AL wants as clients.
- **Sporting hospitality** — Harlequins, West Ham, cricket; roughly 1 match/month.
- **LinkedIn** — explicit content split: Jon covers commercial, retrofit, creative-education;
  Oliver covers regeneration, living, AI. The automation tool previously used (Zavari) was
  flagged by LinkedIn as automation and is not reliable.
- **White papers as lead magnets** — the CO-LIV co-living white paper, a Student Resi re-release,
  and **SiteMAXXER** (a site-viability app) as a lead magnet.
- **Substack newsletter** — semi-automated; Oliver on the auto-generated drafts: *"I just never
  like what it comes up with."* Platform choice (Substack vs Beehiiv) undecided as of the most
  recent check.
- **BE:AI Supper Club** — Oliver and Jon host a dinner series convening founders and
  practitioners "trialling and rolling out software, platforms, and digital solutions" for
  built-environment problems. A genuinely distinct warm-inbound channel, not outreach-driven.
- **Referral / "Hunting Partners"** — see `context/clients.md`.
